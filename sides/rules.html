Playing together

Community rules

Keep multiplayer sessions fair, welcoming, and enjoyable for everyone.

  1. 01

    Fun is allowed but not to much

    On real Servers that are not made by you or your friend group LAN Utilities should not be used, though it can be used to troll your friends.

  2. 02

    Play fairly

    Do not use the mod or a multiplayer session to disrupt or unfairly disadvantage others, if you use it to gain an competetive advantage over others you will face consequences and we will not be responsible.

  3. 03

    Follow the Servers guidelines

    Server Owners may set additional rules for their own sessions.

  4. 04

    Share responsibly

    Download the mod from trusted sources and do not redistribute modified files as official releases.
