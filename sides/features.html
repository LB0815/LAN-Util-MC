In-game toolkit

LAN Utility features

A configurable collection of movement, world, combat, and display utilities for Minecraft.

01

Movement & camera

Adjust how you move through the world and view your surroundings.

  • Freecam
  • Fly and always sprint
  • Adjustable hyper-speed, hyper-jump, and hyper-swim
  • Anti-slow for cobwebs, soul sand, and ice
  • Scale, Stepheight Modifiers
02

World utilities

Convenient controls for interacting with blocks and coordinates.

  • Teleport using coordinates
  • Auto-mine and auto-place
  • Choose a custom block for placement
  • Outline nearby ores with an adjustable range
03

Combat & protection

Optional automation and survival settings, configurable in-game.

  • Auto-target, auto-aim, and auto-crit
  • Choose between players, mobs, and items as targets
  • Auto-totem
  • Anti-fire, no fall damage, and no damage options
04

Display & tuning

Adjust visibility and fine-tune supported settings to your preference.

  • Gamma control
  • Optional health, armor, food, and saturation info
  • Adjustable target interval
  • Configurable movement values
  • Optional Mod Indicator
  • Armor Hud
Features that affect gameplay should only be used in worlds or servers where the host allows them. Always follow the server's rules.
